# Nimbus Notes - Support FAQ

Nimbus Notes is a note-taking and sync app. This FAQ covers the questions our support team answers most often, grouped by topic.

## Accounts and sign-in

**How do I reset my password?**
Open the sign-in page, select "Forgot password," and enter the email on your account. We send a reset link that stays valid for 60 minutes. If it expires, request a new one from the same page.

**Can I change the email address on my account?**
Yes. Go to Settings, then Account, then Email. Enter the new address and confirm it from the verification message we send. Your notes, folders, and settings stay exactly as they are.

**Why am I not receiving the verification email?**
Check your spam folder first. If it is not there, confirm the address is spelled correctly and request the email again. Delivery usually takes under a minute but can take up to 10.

## Billing and plans

**What plans does Nimbus Notes offer?**
Three: Free, Personal, and Team. Free covers one device and 1 GB of storage. Personal covers unlimited devices and 50 GB. Team adds shared workspaces and 200 GB per member.

**What is your refund policy?**
We offer a full refund within 30 days of any payment, no questions asked. Contact support with your account email and we process the refund to your original payment method within five business days.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Go to Settings, then Billing, then Cancel plan. Your paid features stay active until the end of the current billing period, and you are not charged again after that.

**Do you charge for extra storage?**
Personal and Team plans can add storage in 50 GB blocks for a monthly fee. You can remove a block at any time, and the change applies from your next billing date.

## Syncing and devices

**How does syncing work?**
Notes sync automatically whenever your device is online, and changes appear on your other devices within a few seconds. There is no manual sync button because sync runs continuously in the background.

**What happens if I edit the same note on two devices at once?**
Nimbus Notes keeps both versions and marks the note as conflicted, so nothing is lost. You can pick which version to keep or merge them by hand.

**Is there an offline mode?**
Yes. You can read and edit notes offline, and your changes sync the next time your device connects. Offline editing is available on every plan, including Free.

## Data and privacy

**Where is my data stored?**
Your notes are encrypted at rest. Only you and the people you explicitly share a note with can read its contents.

**Can I export all my notes?**
Yes. Go to Settings, then Export, and choose Markdown or PDF. We generate a single archive and email you a download link that stays valid for 24 hours.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your notes after a 14-day grace period, during which you can restore the account by signing back in.
